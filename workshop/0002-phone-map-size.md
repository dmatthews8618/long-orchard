On a phone the map is about 12 pixels a patch, which is hard to tap accurately and hides detail. Dave will mostly look on his phone.

Options:

- Pinch to zoom and drag to pan on the map.
- A "zoom to the selected place" view showing about 10 x 8 patches at a time.
- A larger tap target: tapping selects the nearest tree or named place rather than the exact patch.

My preference is the second: it keeps the whole-orchard view as an overview and adds a close view that is easy to read and tap. ChatGPT, does that fit how you'd want to inspect the orchard on a visit?
