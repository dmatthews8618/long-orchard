# Recruitment: from seed to flowering tree

Development worlds only (seeds 101, 102, 103, 104), 140 orchard years each: about one Bellfruit lifetime. Engine 0.1.1. Generated by `tools/recruitment.mjs` and `tools/recruitment-report.mjs`; the tracer only listens to the engine and does not change the worlds.

Sites are judged on the day a seed germinates: under the crown of a living Bellfruit or Lantern Pear, in a gap (ground once under a crown that has since died, or on more than 5 kg of deadwood), or open ground elsewhere.

## What this shows

**Bellfruit does not renew itself.** In three of four worlds it is gone within 80 to 110 years. In the fourth, a single orchard-born tree is left at year 140. Lantern Pear loses every founding tree too, but replaces itself through five generations.

**The good openings work.** Bellfruit seedlings that start in gaps or on deadwood establish 44% of the time, and in open ground 67 to 75%. Under a living Bellfruit the figure is 9 to 16%, and almost none of those reach flowering: young trees under their parents starve before a gap opens. So favourable patches do let children through.

**Seeds almost never reach those openings alive.** Mice take 93 to 95% of the Bellfruit seed the orchard makes. They do carry it into the open (in one world, over 8 years: about 4,200 seeds cached in grass and 1,000 on deadwood). But they cache where they live, in the densest cover, and they dig those caches back up. Of the seeds cached in grass, about 1% are still there by spring; on deadwood, about 2%. Caches under the canopy, where mice are fewer, keep about 11%, and those germinate into the shade that kills them. Across all four worlds, over 2,000 orchard-born Bellfruit germinated under Bellfruit, 25 in gaps and 4 in the open.

**The blockage is the route, not the seedbed.** The failure sits in two linked places: cache recovery that depends only on how many mice live nearby, so caches in the best ground are nearly always found; and young trees under a canopy that cannot wait for a gap.

## Engine fixes made before these runs (engine 0.1.1)

- On frost days a seedling faced two mortality draws at the full combined hazard, and any death from the first was labelled frost. It now faces one draw a day, and the recorded cause is chosen in proportion to each pressure. (Found by ChatGPT reading the code.)
- The seasonal tidy-up of tiny seed cohorts could pool orchard-born seed into a founding-stock cohort. Founding stock and orchard-born seed are now pooled separately, so the founding seed bank's story stays its own.
